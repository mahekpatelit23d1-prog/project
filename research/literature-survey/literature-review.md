# Literature Review 

## Purpose 

This document records existing research, papers, and projects related to the team's AI/ML project. 

## Research Topics 

- Startup funding prediction 
- Startup valuation prediction 
- Startup success prediction 
- Machine learning for startup analysis 

## Research 

(1) Paper / Project : How to succeed in the market? Predicting startup success using a  machine learning approach

    Year :  2023

    Problem : Predict startup success

    Dataset : 218,207 companies from Crunchbase 

    ML Approach :  Six ML models 

    Six ML models : Examined internal and industry characteristics related to startup success

    Source : ScienceDirect

(2) Paper / Project :  Predicting the Success of a Startup in Information Technology Through Machine Learning

    Year :  2023

    Problem : Predict IT startup success

    Dataset : 265 Australian IT startups 

    ML Approach :  Seven ML algorithms and hybrid models  

    Six ML models : Investigated multiple success factors and ML approaches for startup prediction 

    Source : ScienceDirect


## Literature Findings

The reviewed studies show that machine learning can be used to investigate and predict startup success.

Commonly investigated factors include:

- Funding and investment
- Company characteristics
- Industry or sector
- Founder-related characteristics
- Market-related factors
- Startup age and development stage

The studies also demonstrate the use of multiple classification and machine-learning algorithms rather than relying on a single approach.

## Relevance to Project

The findings are relevant to the current project because the dataset also contains startup characteristics such as:

- Startup age
- Funding rounds
- Founder experience
- Team size
- Market size
- Product traction
- Revenue
- Investor type
- Sector
- Founder background

The literature provides a research basis for investigating these variables when predicting the 'Success' target.
